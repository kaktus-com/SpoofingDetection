detect2 / synthetic observations 01

Benign and single-transmitter carrier phase

Both cases use the same antenna motion, signal wavelengths and slow phase trends. Benign signals follow eight different directions; spoofed signals share one physical direction. Each case has its own repeatable noise realization. These are generated observations, not RF samples or detector results.

“Known truth trend” is the exact quadratic used by the generator, removed only for these comparison plots. It is unavailable to the future detector. The input CSVs retain the full observed phase. No trend estimator, classifier, likelihood test or detection accuracy is computed here.
