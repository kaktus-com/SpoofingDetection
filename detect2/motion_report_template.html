IMU displacement versus held-out truth

Specific force is rotated into ENU with supplied attitude, gravity is removed, and acceleration is integrated with zero initial position and velocity in each window. The rotating antenna offset is added before sampling at GNSS epochs. Truth is accessed only after IMU predictions have been saved.

Unknown initial velocity gives linear drift in raw displacement. “Observable” here means after the same quadratic removal used by the detector, not a correction available to full navigation. No true initial velocity, position, bias or generated phase trend initializes the IMU estimate. Attitude is supplied, not estimated.
