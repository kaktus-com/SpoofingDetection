usage: runtime.py [-h] [--output-root OUTPUT_ROOT] [--duration-s DURATION_S]
                  [--gps-device GPS_DEVICE] [--mavlink-bind MAVLINK_BIND]
                  [--timesync] [--finalize FINALIZE]

Configure at runtime, record original streams and collect the native ULog. No
startup files, receiver flash/BBR writes, router starts, reboot or arming. PX4
logging parameters may be autosaved by PX4 if correction is necessary.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --output-root OUTPUT_ROOT
  --duration-s DURATION_S
                        Recording seconds after required streams are ready;
                        default: until Ctrl+C
  --gps-device GPS_DEVICE
  --mavlink-bind MAVLINK_BIND
  --timesync            Estimate Pi/PX4 clocks with addressed TIMESYNC; GNSS
                        clock mapping remains unverified
  --finalize FINALIZE   Collect the bookmarked native log after disarm; no
                        receiver reconfiguration
